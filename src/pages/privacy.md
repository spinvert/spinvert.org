# Privacy Policy

## Summary

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising. It does not load any content from third parties: fonts, images and scripts are all served from this website itself. The only personal data processed is the technical data that is necessary to deliver the website to your browser (see [Hosting](#hosting)) and data you send us yourself when you contact us.

## Controller

The controller responsible for data processing on this website within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Libre Solar Technologies GmbH. The full address and contact details can be found in the [imprint](/imprint/).

## Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you visit this website, GitHub processes technical data that your browser transmits automatically in order to deliver the pages and to keep the service secure. This includes in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested page, the referring website and information about your browser and operating system. This data is not combined with other data sources by us, and we do not have access to it in a form that identifies you.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the reliable and secure delivery of this website.

GitHub may process data in the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, on the basis of which the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Further information can be found in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Local storage in your browser

When you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in the local storage of your browser so that it is kept on your next visit. This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time via your browser settings. Storing this information is strictly necessary to provide the function you requested (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

## Contacting us

If you contact us by email or phone, we process the data you provide (e.g. name, email address, phone number and the content of your message) in order to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures, and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR otherwise. Our legitimate interest is answering your request. We delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed for this purpose, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

## Links to other websites

This website contains links to other websites, for example to GitHub. Data is only transmitted to these websites once you click a link. From then on, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
- Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
- Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
- Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
- Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
- Right to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR)

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details in the [imprint](/imprint/).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the [Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information](https://datenschutz-hamburg.de/).
