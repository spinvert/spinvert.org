The **Spinvert Boost 4820** is an open-source active rectifier and DC/DC boost converter with
48 V output for 3-phase permanent magnet generators. Its main purpose is to improve the
performance of small wind turbines at low wind speeds, but it is suitable for any 3-phase
permanent magnet generator, e.g. in micro hydro power plants or pedal power generators.

The hardware is based on the [Libre Solar](https://libre.solar) MPPT charge controllers and will
be supported by the Libre Solar charge controller firmware.

<Callout type="caution" title="Early development stage">
  The project is at a very early stage of development (alpha). Major issues are expected, and the
  documentation describes the intended design, which may still change.
</Callout>

## How it works

A small wind turbine produces a voltage roughly proportional to its speed. At low wind speeds,
this voltage is too low to charge a 48 V battery, so a turbine connected directly to the battery
via a diode rectifier produces nothing until it spins fast enough. The Spinvert Boost solves this
in three stages:

1. An **active rectifier** converts the 3-phase AC current from the generator to DC with much
   lower losses than a diode bridge.
2. A **DC/DC boost converter** steps the rectified voltage up to the battery voltage. It controls
   the current drawn from the generator so that the turbine operates at its optimum speed for
   the current wind conditions.
3. At high wind speeds, the rectified voltage is high enough that boosting is not needed anymore.
   A **bypass** then connects the rectifier directly to the output.

A **dump load** behind the rectifier keeps the generator loaded if the battery is full or the
turbine runs too fast.

## Specifications

| Parameter                  | Value                                     |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Generator type             | 3-phase permanent magnet generator        |
| Input voltage (rectified)  | 0 to 60 V                                 |
| Rectifier current          | max. 20 A                                 |
| Output voltage             | 48 V nominal (battery or DC bus)          |
| Boost converter            | max. 10 A output (approx. 480 W)          |
| Bypass                     | max. 20 A output (approx. 960 W)          |
| Dump load output           | max. 20 A                                 |
| Boost inductor / frequency | 47 µH / 128 kHz                           |
| Interfaces                 | USB (monitoring, debugging), CAN-FD       |
| Firmware                   | Libre Solar charge controller firmware    |
