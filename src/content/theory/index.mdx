This section explains the background behind the Spinvert hardware: how small wind turbines,
micro hydro turbines and pedal generators convert mechanical power to electricity, and how this
power is transferred to a battery as efficiently as possible. The content is independent of a
specific product. For the Spinvert Boost 4820, see the [hardware documentation](/hardware/boost-4820/).
